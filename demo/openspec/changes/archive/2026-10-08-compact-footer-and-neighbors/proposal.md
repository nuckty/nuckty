# Compact branded footer and directional neighboring links

## Why
The footer lacks hierarchy and the neighboring links repeat direction labels. On the website, the sections button duplicates the hamburger.

## What Changes
Use a compact brand/navigation/license footer with short link labels and stable mobile rows. Render neighboring page names with outward chevrons and no visible previous/next captions. Remove the redundant website sections button while retaining static fallback navigation.

## Impact
Consumer templates/CSS and website search controller.
