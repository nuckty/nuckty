# Clarify mobile theme controls and article entry

## Why
The theme icon is easy to overlook on phones. Small breadcrumbs duplicate long article titles, and the section strip adds excessive space above the text.

## What Changes
Give theme, search and mobile menu controls clear icons and touch targets. Retain localStorage persistence and move the mobile language entry into the main menu. Show a readable ancestor trail without repeating the article heading, and tighten the mobile section strip and title spacing.

## Impact
Consumer root templates, shared CSS and theme control labels.
